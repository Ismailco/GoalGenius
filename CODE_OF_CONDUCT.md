# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Rungset is a small open-source project, and everyone participating in it is
expected to help keep the community welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints, identities, and levels of experience.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus on the work and its impact rather than on people.
- Protect user privacy: never share credentials, private workspace data, or
  other sensitive information in issues, pull requests, or screenshots.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure
of private information, trolling, and other conduct that would make a
reasonable participant feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact
`contact@ismailcourr.dev` with the relevant context. Reports will be reviewed
as privately as possible. A maintainer may remove content, restrict
participation, or take other action needed to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and project-related events.
